My Reflections on The Richest Man in Babylon

Chapter 1: 7 Cures for a Lean Purse

In this chapter, I learned that improving my financial situation requires discipline, planning, and consistent habits. The lesson that stood out to me is the importance of saving a portion of the money I receive instead of spending everything. As a university student, I can apply this by setting aside some of my money, planning my expenses, and thinking carefully before making purchases.

My biggest takeaway from this chapter is that financial improvement begins with small, consistent decisions. I want to develop responsible money habits now so that I can manage my finances better in the future.

Chapter 2: 5 Laws of Gold

This chapter encouraged me to think about how money can be earned, protected, and grown through wise decisions. I learned the importance of making informed financial choices instead of taking unnecessary risks or following advice without understanding it. As I continue my education, I want to learn more about budgeting, saving, and making responsible decisions with money.

My biggest takeaway is that building financial security requires patience, knowledge, and good judgement. I can apply these lessons by setting financial goals, learning before making important decisions, and avoiding spending simply to satisfy temporary wants.

How I Will Apply These Lessons

Both chapters remind me that personal growth involves developing habits that support my future. I plan to practise saving regularly, create a realistic budget, distinguish between needs and wants, and review my progress towards my financial goals. These are habits I can begin developing while I am still a student.
