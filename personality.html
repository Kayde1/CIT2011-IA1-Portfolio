My Interests and Passions

I enjoy activities that allow me to express myself, explore ideas, and learn something new. I am particularly interested in technology, business, creativity, and lifestyle content. These interests give me opportunities to discover new perspectives and develop my own ideas.

My Personal Strengths

I value creativity because it encourages me to approach ideas in different ways. I am also willing to learn, accept new challenges, and work towards improving myself. I believe that patience, determination, and an open mind are important when facing difficulties.

My Values and Attitude

I believe in continuous improvement, responsibility, and treating others with respect. I value opportunities to learn from my experiences, listen to different viewpoints, and use feedback to improve. I also believe that progress comes from being consistent and willing to try.

How I Approach Challenges

When I face a challenge, I aim to understand the problem, consider possible solutions, and learn from the outcome. I recognise that mistakes can be opportunities to grow. I want to become more confident in my decisions and develop the ability to adapt when circumstances change.

My Hero and Role Model

My hero and role model is myself. I believe that becoming the person I want to be starts with believing in my own potential, recognising my strengths, and learning from my mistakes. My journey as a Computer Information Systems student is helping me discover what I am capable of and the kind of future I want to create.

I look up to myself because I continue to work towards my goals, challenge myself to learn new things, and strive to become a better version of who I was yesterday. Although I know I still have much to learn, I believe that my experiences, determination, and willingness to grow will help shape my future. To me, being my own role model means taking responsibility for my choices, celebrating my progress, and never giving up on my potential.

My Personal Motto

Dream Big. Create Boldly. Grow Continuously.

AI as a Learning Tool, Not a Crutch

As a student, I believe that large language models (LLMs) such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Copilot should be used to support learning rather than replace my own effort. These tools can explain difficult concepts, provide examples, help me brainstorm ideas, and guide me when I am unsure how to approach a problem. For example, when learning HTML and CSS, an AI tool can help me understand what a tag does or explain why a webpage is not displaying correctly.

However, relying on AI to complete every assignment can prevent me from developing my own knowledge, critical-thinking skills, creativity, and problem-solving abilities. AI can also provide inaccurate information, so I must check important facts against reliable sources. I should never submit AI-generated work without understanding it or following my lecturer's academic integrity guidelines.

My approach is to attempt tasks independently first, use AI when I need guidance, verify the information, and then apply what I have learned in my own work. Ultimately, AI should be a learning assistant that helps me become more capable and independent, not a shortcut that does my learning for me.
